# RecoverFlow — First Customer Sales Playbook

## Positioning

RecoverFlow is a receivables operations workspace for Indian B2B businesses.

Core promise:
> See outstanding cash, take the next collection action, and keep the whole team on the same workflow.

Lead with the cash problem, not the feature list.

## Initial ICP

Start with B2B service firms that:
- send recurring or milestone invoices;
- follow up through spreadsheets, email or WhatsApp;
- have a founder, finance lead or operations lead who can approve a pilot quickly.

Recommended first segments:
1. software development studios
2. digital marketing agencies
3. IT consulting firms
4. design/creative agencies
5. professional-services firms

## Offer

### Founder-led 14-day pilot

Give the prospect a live workspace using a small slice of their real receivables.

Pilot setup:
1. Import 10–50 open invoices.
2. Review aging and customer exposure.
3. Create payment links for selected invoices.
4. Run follow-up workflows.
5. Review what changed at the end of the pilot.
6. Convert the same workspace to a paid plan.

Do not promise a specific recovery amount.

## Discovery questions

Ask:
1. How many open invoices do you normally carry?
2. How do you currently track overdue accounts?
3. Which channel do you use for reminders?
4. Who owns follow-up when an invoice becomes overdue?
5. How do you know which customers need attention today?
6. What happens when a customer pays partially?
7. What would make you keep a collections tool after a 14-day pilot?

## Demo sequence

Do not start with settings.

1. Show the collection dashboard.
2. Open one overdue invoice.
3. Show customer context.
4. Create a payment link.
5. Show the follow-up workflow.
6. Show the customer statement.
7. Show recurring billing.
8. Show the Android app.
9. Show the team/audit controls.
10. Ask what they would replace with RecoverFlow.

## Conversion

At the end of the pilot:

> “You already have the workspace running. Would you like to keep it active on the Starter, Business or Pro plan?”

Do not discount before hearing an objection.

## Objection handling

### “We already use Tally/Zoho/accounting software.”

Response:
> That makes sense. RecoverFlow is not positioned as your accounting system. It is the collection operations layer around the receivables that still need human follow-up.

### “We only have a few invoices.”

Response:
> Then the pilot should be easy to evaluate. Start with the invoices that are currently difficult to collect and see whether the workflow helps.

### “Why should we pay for reminders?”

Response:
> The product is not just reminders. The value is the workflow around the receivable: ownership, next action, payment link, customer context, team visibility and follow-up history.

### “Send me details.”

Response:
> I’ll send the 1-minute overview and a demo link. The useful part is seeing it on an actual receivables workflow, so the demo is intentionally short.

## First-customer target

Do not optimize for a large top-of-funnel number.

Target:
- 25 highly relevant prospects
- 10 conversations
- 5 pilots
- first 1–3 paid workspaces

These are internal operating targets, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Daily founder routine

Every sales day:
- add 5 qualified prospects;
- send 5 personalized first touches;
- follow up with previous conversations;
- run demos;
- record objections;
- update lead status in RecoverFlow.

## What to learn before scaling

Track why prospects:
- reply;
- book a demo;
- start a pilot;
- activate payment workflows;
- upgrade;
- cancel.

Use those objections to change positioning and product onboarding before increasing outreach volume.

## Current commercial product flow

Website
→ 14-day trial or demo request
→ onboarding
→ invoice import
→ collection workflow
→ pilot result
→ paid subscription
→ recurring billing

## Competitive context

The current Indian market already includes products positioning around WhatsApp/AI-powered collections and MSME workflow automation. RecoverFlow should therefore avoid sounding like “just another WhatsApp reminder tool” and emphasize its receivables operating workflow, team access, customer portfolio, payment actions and auditability.

## Launch checklist

Before charging real customers:
- production Razorpay account configured and test subscription verified;
- real legal entity/support details published;
- paid production database selected;
- support process defined;
- Android production/development build tested on a physical phone;
- first 25 prospects loaded into the sales tracker;
- first demos scheduled;
- subscription cancellation/refund process tested.
