Privacy Policy

RecoverFlow processes the data needed to operate your receivables workspace.

Information we process

Account details, workspace activity, invoice records, customer contact details, audit events and integration configuration required to provide the service.

Payment information

Payment checkout and subscription billing are handled through configured payment providers. RecoverFlow is designed not to store customer card or bank credentials in its own database.

Use of data

Data is used to provide the product, secure accounts, operate integrations, support customers and maintain service records.

Security

Workspace separation, role-based access and encrypted credential storage are used in the current application architecture.

Your controls

Account or data requests can be handled through the service operator's support channel.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

A baseline policy for RecoverFlow subscriptions.

Free trial

RecoverFlow currently offers a 14-day evaluation period. No subscription payment is required to begin the trial.

Cancellation

Customers can stop future renewal through the configured billing flow. Renewal and access behavior depends on the payment-provider subscription state.

Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed based on the billing event, plan and applicable law. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment route where supported.

Launch checklist

Before public paid launch, the operator should add the legal entity name, support contact, registered address, jurisdiction and any required statutory disclosures to these policies.
